Restaurants, caterers and meal-prep shops put a MealProof QR code on the lid, bag or label as the food is made.

You decide

Questions people ask

Do I need to download an app?

No. Scan the code with your phone’s regular camera and the details open in your browser.

Does it cost anything to scan?

No. Scanning is free for diners. Businesses add MealProof codes to the food they serve.

Who enters the information I see?

The restaurant or food business records it as the meal is prepared, stored and packed. MealProof turns that record into a page you can check.

Does MealProof replace my own judgment?

No. It gives you clearer information to decide with. If food looks, smells or tastes off, don’t eat it.
